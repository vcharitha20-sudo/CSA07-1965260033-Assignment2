IPv4 fragmentation
Original 4000 bytes includes 20-byte header: payload 3980. MTU 1500 permits 1480 payload per full fragment. Fragment 1: total 1500, offset 0, MF=1. Fragment 2: total 1500, offset 185 (1480/8), MF=1. Fragment 3: payload 1020, total 1040, offset 370, MF=0. Offsets are in eight-byte units.

Evaluation
Path MTU Discovery avoids fragmentation and reassembly overhead; blocked ICMP fragmentation-needed messages can create a PMTU black hole. Datagram packets may take different routes and arrive out of order; virtual circuits typically establish a common forwarding path. DHCP could assign correct mask/gateway and avoid duplicate or invalid host assignments, but cannot repair a wrong upstream design. For 180 simultaneous hosts, /24 gives 254 usable addresses; 300 registered users can share leases only if no more than 254 leases remain active, including stale leases.
