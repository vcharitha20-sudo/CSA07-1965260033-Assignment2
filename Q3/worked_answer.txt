Failover
Primary static default route administrative distance 1; backup floating default route administrative distance 250. When G0/1 goes down, the primary route disappears and the backup becomes eligible, provided its next hop is reachable. When restored, primary wins again. A physical link remaining up while the ISP fails upstream may not trigger basic static-route failover. IP SLA with 5-second probes and 3 missed replies typically detects failure in roughly 10–15 seconds plus scheduling/reaction overhead; link-down failover is usually faster when the interface itself fails.
